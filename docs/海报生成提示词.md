# 海报生成提示词

生成方式：内置 ImageGen（非 CLI）。三张图均为独立生成、原创的纸刊风格视觉。

## 01 / Cobalt

```text
Tall vertical 3:5 phone-poster on a full-frame warm aged-paper surface, 78% clear paper with a small lower-left collage cluster. An original stylized editorial portrait of Chinese singer Shan Yichun appears as a tiny grayscale torn-photocopy clipping, soft halftone and risograph grain, archival and memory-like. Do not copy or reference any existing photograph.

Add only sparse near-illegible typewriter microtext; no readable lyrics, logos, brand name, or watermark. One fully saturated cobalt-blue irregular risograph paper cutout occupies about 20% of the collage cluster. Slight ink bleed and print misregistration. Flat orthographic scanned-paper view, matte fibers, diffuse light, quiet Chinese indie zine mood. Avoid full-bleed photography, commercial campaign, clean digital UI, 3D, cinematic lighting, neon, anime, cute cartoon, dense scrapbook, extra colors, and long text.
```

Recipe：`lower-left-float / tiny faded photo / archive microtext / cobalt cutout / risograph grain / memory`

## 02 / Vermilion

```text
Tall vertical 3:5 aged-paper poster with 75% empty warm ivory paper, a compact upper-right dual-panel collage. An original stylized editorial portrait of Chinese singer Shan Yichun is seated by a window holding a small paper photograph. Do not copy or reference any existing photograph. Use a tiny low-contrast grayscale xerox photo clipping paired with a second torn translucent paper panel.

Include quiet fragmented letterpress marks only; no readable lyric text, logos, brand name, or watermark. A fully saturated tomato-red printed rectangle occupies 20% of the collage cluster; visible at thumbnail size. Risograph grain, scan noise, and a slight registration offset. Flat scanned-paper appearance, diffuse light, nostalgic evening mood. Avoid full-bleed scene, commercial headline, glossy mockup, clean UI, 3D, cinematic lighting, neon, anime, cute cartoon, dense scrapbook, too many colors, long text.
```

Recipe：`upper-right-block / dual photo panel / fragmented letters / tomato-red block / scan noise / evening`

## 03 / Ultramarine

```text
Tall vertical 3:5 phone-poster on warm aged paper. Keep 80% empty paper; place a lower-left floating collage of a narrow grayscale photocopy portrait of an original stylized Chinese female vocalist in profile with eyes closed, holding a small bouquet. Do not copy or reference any existing photograph. Use a torn edge, a single pressed flower, and an irregular paper fragment.

No readable lyrics, logos, brand name, or watermark. Use one vivid ultramarine-blue flat printed cutout at about 25% of the small collage cluster, visibly saturated at thumbnail size. Include light halftone, xerox wear, paper fibers, and imperfect letterpress marks. Flat scanned-paper image with gentle diffuse light, quiet memory and nighttime mood. Avoid full-bleed subject, commercial ad, glossy mockup, clean UI, 3D, cinematic lighting, neon, anime, cute cartoon, dense scrapbook, too many colors, or long clean text.
```

Recipe：`lower-left-float / torn photocopy / almost textless / ultramarine cutout / xerox wear / night`
